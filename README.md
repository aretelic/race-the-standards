# Race the Standards

**Prototype**: an early, mobile-first idea for Leander Swimming Club (Tooting, London), not a finished product.

A swimmer enters their age, gender, pool length, stroke and personal best (PB). An animated race shows which qualifying times they've already made and how much time they need to find for the next one. Each swimmer does the right stroke, and "you" wear a Leander hat.

Leander's pathway runs from Surrey (our county) to the London Region and then Swim England nationals, so the tool uses:

- **County:** Surrey County Age Group Championships 2027 (v1), qualifying and consideration times. Age is taken at 31 December 2027.
- **London regional:** Swim England London Region Summer Championships 2026 qualifying times, with separate tables for 25m and 50m pool times.
- **National:** Swim England National Winter Championships (25m) 2026 qualifying times. These apply to times from either pool length; minimum age 11.

The 2027 London Region and national times aren't published yet, so the 2026 times stand in until they are. The National Summer Meet is invitation-only (by rankings), so it has no qualifying times to show. Always check the official meet pack before entering.

It's a single file (`index.html`) with no dependencies. Open it in any browser.
